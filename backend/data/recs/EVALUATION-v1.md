# How well the recommender works

Generated by `scripts/recs/evaluate.js --final` on 2026-10-20 · MovieLens ml-32m (ratings Jan 1995 – Oct 2023) · 826 of 855 catalogue films found in MovieLens · Node v20.20.2

## How it was tested

- People are split by id: 80% train the counts, 10% choose the settings (validation), 10% are kept for this report (test). Nobody's ratings are used to score them.
- Each test person is rewound to just before the last film they loved (4+ stars). Ratings after that moment are ignored.
- Each method gets their earlier loved films and ranks every catalogue film they hadn't rated yet. A **hit** is the hidden film in the top 10.
- **NDCG@10** also rewards rank: a hit at #1 scores 1, at #10 scores 0.29.
- Settings chosen on validation: λ = 25 (shrinkage), β = 0.1 (content), γ = 0 (canon nudge), 50 neighbours per film (validation NDCG 0.195). Every canon nudge tried made predictions worse, so the engine uses the canon only to break ties and when it has nothing else to go on.
- ± is a 95% interval for sampling noise. "New minus" rows compare methods on the same people.

## Limits

- MovieLens people are not this site's visitors; the numbers say how well the engine predicts film lovers in general.
- Other people's ratings made after a test person's hidden moment still feed the counts. This is standard for this kind of test and slightly flatters every count-based method alike.
- 29 catalogue films aren't in MovieLens (mostly 2023 and later). They are recommended from content alone and can't be tested here.
- On the site, a favourite or a 4–5 star rating counts fully and a watched or watchlisted film counts half. Those weights are a product choice; MovieLens has no equivalent to test them on.
- The counts were refitted on everyone (train + validation + test) for the shipped model, so it has slightly more data than the one tested here.
