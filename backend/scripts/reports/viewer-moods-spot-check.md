8 Nov 2026. The rule (3+ different viewers, at least 3% of the film's taggers) and the 21 moods were fixed before the checks. The answers are in `data/moods/spot-check-answers.json`.

**Precision: 61 of 61.** With no misses in 61, the true precision is at least about 95% (95% confidence, rule of three).

**What it misses:** the owner marked 12 obvious moods missing across the first sheet's 15 films (existential 3, melancholic 2, poetic 2; intimate, dreamlike, enigmatic, bleak, psychological 1 each). That is the price of a strict rule: what's shown is right, and some films carry fewer moods than they deserve.

**Limits, said plainly:** one judge; films the owner had seen, so better-known ones; romantic checked from a list rather than the sheet.
